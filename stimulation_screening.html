Screening rule: a well is flagged when, on its last day of oil production, the daily average downhole pressure is below 150 bar and the oil rate is below 500 BOPD. This is a simple first-pass heuristic, not a skin or productivity-index calculation. Days where the downhole gauge reported 0 or nothing are treated as missing data and shown as gaps.

Data: Volve field daily production, Equinor open data release.
