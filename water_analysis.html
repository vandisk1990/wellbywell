Chan plot method: Chan, K.S. (1995), "Water Control Diagnostic Plots", SPE 30775. WOR = water/oil on days with both phases flowing; time is cumulative producing days. WOR' = dWOR/dt from a centred difference over ±15 producing days on a 31-day moving average of WOR, so daily noise does not swamp the trend. Negative WOR' values are omitted because a log axis cannot show them. A rising WOR' with positive slope suggests coning or channelling; a flat or falling WOR' suggests normal displacement.

Data: Volve field daily production, Equinor open data release.
